## What coverage means

A covered command has a generator provenance record, a public declaration, a source definition, and an observed direct call through Volk's loaded `vk*` function pointer. Alias spellings share one canonical command; they do not imply separate cvk entry points.

The structure map separately records direct parameters, nested members, and registry `pNext` relationships, plus observed builders and command/extender conveniences. A builder is not proof of a command-specific convenience. Conveniences are indexed by command and extender, not by every possible nested path.

This measures the repository's API surface, not Vulkan conformance or complete specification semantics. It does not test driver behavior, feature enablement, all chain combinations, opaque payloads, callback signatures, or valid usage. The map includes all generated platform files; a particular CMake build selects only its platform sources ([CMakeLists.txt](../CMakeLists.txt)). Vulkan SC is separate.
